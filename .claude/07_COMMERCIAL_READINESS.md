# Commercial Readiness

## Before monetization

For every data source verify:
- commercial use
- display rights
- redistribution
- derived data
- caching
- retention
- deletion
- attribution
- branding
- application links
- rate limits
- acceptable use

## Product compliance

Prepare:
- privacy policy
- terms of use
- cookie/analytics disclosures where applicable
- source attribution
- data methodology
- contact/support path
- deletion/correction process where applicable

## Commercial data transition

```text
Free/low-volume MVP
       ↓
Validate product
       ↓
Negotiate/licence feeds
       ↓
Higher-volume ingestion
       ↓
Paid infrastructure only where justified
```

## Do not

- claim complete Australian coverage without evidence
- claim visa sponsorship without reliable source evidence
- present estimates as official statistics
- present derived analytics as government metrics
